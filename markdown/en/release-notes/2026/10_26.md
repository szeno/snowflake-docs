## SQL updates

### Notification integrations for cost anomaly alerts (*General availability*)

You can now send cost anomaly notifications to Slack, SMS (text message), or webhook destinations by registering a notification
integration. New methods in the SNOWFLAKE.LOCAL.ANOMALY\_INSIGHTS class let you add, list, and remove notification integrations for
account-level and organization-level cost anomalies.

For more information, see [Introduction to cost anomalies](/user-guide/cost-anomalies) and [ANOMALY\_INSIGHTS!ADD\_NOTIFICATION\_INTEGRATION](/sql-reference/classes/anomaly-insights/methods/add_notification_integration).
