# Oct 28, 2026: Installment payment schedules in Consumer Hub (*General availability*)

With this release, we’re pleased to announce the general availability of installment payment schedules in Consumer Hub. When
you view the details of an accepted private offer that you pay for in installments, Consumer Hub now shows the payment schedule
for the purchase, including the number, amount, and due date of each installment.

To view a payment schedule, in Snowsight, select **Marketplace** » **Consumer Hub**, select the **Private offers** tab, and
then select **View details** for the offer.

For more information, see [View the installment payment schedule for a private offer](/user-guide/collaboration/listings/pricing-plans-offers/consumers-manage-offers#label-consumers-manage-offers-consumer-hub-payment-schedule).
