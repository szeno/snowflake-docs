# Nov 4, 2026: HTML reports (*Preview*)

HTML reports are now available in public preview. Create a self-contained HTML report with CoCo in Snowsight Workspaces or CoCo Desktop, or generate one in Snowflake CoWork with an agent that has code execution enabled. Publish it as a Report artifact to share a snapshot of its content with users or roles in your account. Recipients can open the artifact in Snowsight or CoWork without access to the source workspace or data tables.

Reports support interactive charts based on saved data and links to external sites. To update shared content after editing or refreshing a source report, publish an update to the artifact.

For more information, see [HTML reports](/user-guide/ui-snowsight/reports) and [Create and share a dashboard or HTML report](/user-guide/snowflake-artifacts-create-share).
