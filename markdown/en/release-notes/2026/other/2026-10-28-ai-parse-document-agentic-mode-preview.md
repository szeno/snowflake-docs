# Oct 28, 2026: AI\_PARSE\_DOCUMENT introduces AGENTIC mode for intelligent document processing (*Public Preview*)

Snowflake Cortex AI\_PARSE\_DOCUMENT introduces AGENTIC mode in Public Preview, turning complex enterprise
documents—from financial reports to technical manuals—into actionable information for AI applications,
analytics, and automation. Go beyond text extraction to unlock information in charts, diagrams, tables,
and other visuals with a single SQL call, without building or managing complex document-processing pipelines.

Key use cases include:

- **Intelligent document processing:** Transform documents containing text, tables, and visuals into usable
  information for business processes and AI applications.
- **Agentic document workflows:** Automate document interpretation and processing, reducing manual effort
  and custom engineering.
- **Multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG):** Make information embedded in charts, diagrams, and
  screenshots accessible to enterprise search and RAG applications.
- **Structured data extraction:** Extract chart series and values, table headers and cells, and diagram
  components and relationships for analytics and automation.
- **Enterprise knowledge discovery:** Uncover insights from reports, presentations, and manuals by making
  both textual and visual information accessible to search and AI applications.

AGENTIC mode identifies and classifies visual content, selects and executes processing actions, and generates
semantic summaries, structured data, or both. Snowflake manages the orchestration, returning document text,
layout, visual summaries, and extracted data in a unified response.

For more information, see [Agentic mode](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/parse-document#label-parse-document-agentic-mode).
