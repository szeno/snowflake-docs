# Oct 9, 2026: Agentic Marketplace Discovery (*Public preview*)

Agentic Marketplace Discovery is now available in public preview. On the public Snowflake Marketplace **Discover** page in Snowsight, you can switch between **Agentic discovery** and **Marketplace search**. Describe what you need to CoCo to find and compare matching listings, or search for providers and listings directly.

From the CoCo conversation, you can explore a listing, install it, start a trial, or initiate a sales request when the listing supports that action and your role has the required privileges. You can continue the conversation elsewhere in Snowsight while you evaluate, try, and implement the products you find.

For more information, see [Agentic Marketplace Discovery](/collaboration/marketplace-agentic-search).
