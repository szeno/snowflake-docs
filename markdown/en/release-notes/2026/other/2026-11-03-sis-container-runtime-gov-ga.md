# Nov 3, 2026: Streamlit in Snowflake container runtime in AWS government regions (*General availability*)

The Streamlit in Snowflake container runtime is now generally available in the following AWS government regions:

- US East (Commercial Gov - N. Virginia)
- US West (Commercial Gov - Oregon)
- US Gov East 1 (FedRAMP High Plus)
- US Gov West 1 (FedRAMP High Plus)

Container runtimes run your Streamlit in Snowflake apps on Snowpark Container Services compute pools. The container runtime isn’t yet FedRAMP-authorized.
For account availability and authorization status, contact your Snowflake account team.

For more information, see [Runtime environments for Streamlit apps](/developer-guide/streamlit/app-development/runtime-environments).
