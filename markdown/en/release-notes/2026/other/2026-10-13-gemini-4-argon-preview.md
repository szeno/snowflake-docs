# Oct 13, 2026: Gemini 4 Argon model in AI Functions (*Preview*)

The `gemini-4-argon` model is now available in Public Preview in AI Functions with AI\_COMPLETE.

The model supports cross-region inference with `CORTEX_ENABLED_CROSS_REGION` set to `ANY_REGION` or `GCP_US`.

For more information, see [Models and regional availability for Cortex AI Functions](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/aisql-regional-availability).
