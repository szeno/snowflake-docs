# Aug 14, 2026: Trust Center violation findings remediation (*General availability*)

The following Trust Center findings remediation capabilities are now generally available:

- *One-click remediation*: for supported CIS Benchmarks violation findings, the Trust Center generates the SQL statements that remediate
  the violation findings, shows you the plan, and runs it after you approve it. For more information, see
  [Remediate violation findings with one-click remediation](/user-guide/trust-center/using-the-trust-center#label-trust-center-remediate-violations-programmatically).
- *Remediation with CoCo*: CoCo explains a violation finding in the context of your account, recommends remediation steps, and can run
  them with your approval. For more information, see
  [Remediate violation findings with CoCo](/user-guide/trust-center/using-the-trust-center#label-trust-center-remediate-with-cortex-code).
