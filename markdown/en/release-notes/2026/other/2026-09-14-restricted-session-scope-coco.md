# Sep 14, 2026: User-managed Restricted Session Scope in CoCo (*General availability*)

With this release, user-managed Restricted Session Scope (RSS) is generally available in CoCo CLI,
CoCo Desktop, and CoCo in Snowsight.

You can restrict an individual CoCo chat to read-only access or selected roles. The user-managed RSS
can only narrow the privileges available through your roles and any admin-managed RSS that applies
to the session.

For more information, see:
