# Jun 1, 2026: Streamlit in Snowflake in Workspaces (*General availability*)

With this release, Streamlit in Snowflake in Workspaces is now generally available.

Streamlit in Snowflake in Workspaces lets you create, edit, and deploy Streamlit apps directly inside
workspaces in Snowsight. Workspaces provide a file-based development environment
that separates editing from publishing, so your changes don’t affect the live app until
you explicitly deploy.

For more information, see [Streamlit in Snowflake in Workspaces](/developer-guide/streamlit/streamlit-in-workspaces/streamlit-in-workspaces-overview).
