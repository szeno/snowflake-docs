# Nov 16, 2026: AI\_COMPLETE adds support for Twelve Labs Pegasus 1.5 (*General availability*)

Snowflake Cortex AI\_COMPLETE now supports Twelve Labs Pegasus 1.5, a video understanding model designed to analyze and
extract insights from video content. This addition enables organizations to process videos directly in Snowflake using
natural language prompts, making it easier to summarize recordings, identify key events, and extract structured
information from video files without building separate video processing pipelines.

Key capabilities include:

- **Advanced video understanding:** Analyze video content using Pegasus 1.5 to identify scenes, actions, objects, and
  events across recordings.
- **Video summarization:** Generate natural language summaries of video content, helping users quickly understand
  lengthy recordings without watching them in full.
- **Video question answering:** Ask questions about video content using natural language prompts to retrieve relevant
  information and insights.
- **Structured information extraction:** Extract information from videos for downstream analytics, reporting, and
  AI-powered applications.
- **Support for long-form videos:** Process video files up to 2 hours in duration and 2 GB in size, enabling analysis of
  longer recordings.
- **Seamless integration with Snowflake:** Use Pegasus 1.5 through existing AI\_COMPLETE SQL workflows to analyze video
  files stored in Snowflake stages.

Pegasus 1.5 supports MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and WebM video formats.

For more information, see [Cortex AI Functions: Multimodal](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/ai-multimodal).
