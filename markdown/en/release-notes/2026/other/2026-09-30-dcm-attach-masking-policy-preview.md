# Sep 30, 2026: DCM Projects ATTACH MASKING POLICY (*Preview*)

With this Public Preview release, you can use `ATTACH MASKING POLICY` in DCM Projects definition files to declaratively assign a masking policy to one or more columns in tables, Apache Iceberg™ tables, views, or dynamic tables. DCM Projects manages the masking policy attachment throughout its deployment lifecycle.

For more information, see [ATTACH Masking Policy](/user-guide/dcm-projects/dcm-projects-supported-entities#label-dcm-attach-masking-policy).
