# Nov 16, 2026: Openflow Snowflake Deployments in Azure government regions (*General availability*)

Openflow Snowflake Deployments are now generally available in the following Azure government region:

- US Gov Virginia (FedRAMP High Plus)

Openflow isn’t yet FedRAMP-authorized. For account availability and authorization status, contact your
Snowflake account team.

For more information, see [Azure government regions](/user-guide/data-integration/openflow/about-spcs#label-openflow-spcs-azure-government-regions).
