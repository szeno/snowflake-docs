# Oct 21, 2026: Expanded insights for Snowflake Postgres

Snowflake Postgres now offers expanded insights through the `insights` extension. After you create
the extension in a database, it continuously collects performance and health metrics from your
instance and stores them in the `insights` schema. You can view hourly and daily history,
expected-range baselines, and metric trend forecasts on the **Insights** tab in Snowsight, or query the
data directly with SQL.

Expanded insights require Postgres 18.6 or later.

For more information, see [Expanded insights](/user-guide/snowflake-postgres/insights#expanded-insights).
