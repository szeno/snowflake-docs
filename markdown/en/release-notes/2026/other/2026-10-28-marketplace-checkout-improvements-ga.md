# Oct 28, 2026: Snowflake Marketplace checkout improvements (*General availability*)

With this release, we’re pleased to announce the general availability of the following improvements to the
Snowflake Marketplace checkout experience:

- **Checkout for all offers**: The **Complete your purchase** page, previously available only for private offers with
  flat-fee pricing, is now used for private offers with usage-based pricing and for standard offers.
- **Additional invoice recipients**: For flat-fee offers, you can add up to 10 email addresses that receive the
  invoices for a specific purchase. Add recipients when you check out, or later from **Manage Purchase**.
- **MCD limits**: For flat-fee offers, you can limit how much of your
  [Marketplace Capacity Drawdown (MCD)](/collaboration/marketplace-capacity-drawdown) balance is applied to a purchase,
  instead of applying all available MCD funds. Set the limit when you check out, and change it later from
  **Manage Purchase**.
- **Tax exemptions**: If your organization has a sales tax exemption on file with Snowflake, the estimated taxes shown
  at checkout reflect the exemption.
