# Oct 20, 2026: Sensitive Data Entitlement and Access reports (*General availability*)

With this release, the following Trust Center reports are generally available. These reports require
[Enterprise Edition or higher](/user-guide/intro-editions).

- The [Sensitive Data Entitlement report](/user-guide/classify-ui-trust-center#label-classify-trust-center-entitlement-report) lists the
  users, roles, and privileges that grant access to tables containing sensitive data.
- The [Sensitive Data Access report](/user-guide/classify-ui-trust-center#label-classify-trust-center-access-report) lists users who accessed
  tables containing sensitive data during a configurable lookback period.

For more information, see [Use the Trust Center to set up sensitive data classification](/user-guide/classify-ui-trust-center).
