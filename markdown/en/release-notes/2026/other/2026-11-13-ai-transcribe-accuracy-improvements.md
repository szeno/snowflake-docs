# Nov 13, 2026: AI\_TRANSCRIBE delivers improved transcription accuracy

Snowflake Cortex AI\_TRANSCRIBE has been upgraded to deliver improved transcription accuracy across multiple languages,
including English and French. The update improves speech recognition quality, helping produce more accurate
transcripts of meetings, conversations, and recorded audio. These improvements are available automatically, without
requiring any changes to existing SQL queries or transcription workflows.

Key improvements include:

- **Improved transcription accuracy:** Benefit from more accurate speech recognition in key languages, including
  English and French, with fewer word errors compared to the previous version.
- **Better transcription of conversations:** Generate more accurate transcripts from longer recordings, including
  meetings with multiple speakers, interruptions, and natural conversational speech.
- **Enhanced multilingual transcription:** Improved recognition across several supported languages helps
  organizations process multilingual audio more accurately.
- **Higher-quality inputs for AI-powered analysis:** Use more accurate transcripts with other Cortex AI Functions to
  power meeting summarization, content search, conversation analysis, and insight extraction.
- **Seamless upgrade:** Existing AI\_TRANSCRIBE workflows automatically benefit from the improvements without requiring
  changes to SQL queries or application integrations.

For more information, see [AI\_TRANSCRIBE](/sql-reference/functions/ai_transcribe).
