# Nov 19, 2026: AI Governance (*Preview*)

AI Governance is now available in preview. AI Governance is an account-wide page in Snowsight that lets you govern
and audit AI usage in your account. View AI usage metrics for Cortex Code and Cortex Agents, audit agent activity and data
exposure, and review a changelog of changes to your agents. To open it, select **Governance & security** » **AI governance**.

For more information, see [AI Governance](/user-guide/ai-governance).
