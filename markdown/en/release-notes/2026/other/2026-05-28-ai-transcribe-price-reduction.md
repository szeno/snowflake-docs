# May 28, 2026: Planned price reduction for Cortex AI\_TRANSCRIBE

Effective June 1, Snowflake is reducing pricing for Cortex AI\_TRANSCRIBE by up to 60%, making it more
cost-effective to transcribe and analyze audio and video content directly in Snowflake.

With this change, the effective price is targeted to be $0.35 per hour of audio processed for all customers —
compared to previous prices of $0.47 per hour for Standard, $0.70 per hour for Enterprise, and $0.94 per hour
for Business Critical.

AI\_TRANSCRIBE provides SQL-native transcription for audio and video files, helping teams extract spoken
information at scale for analytics, search, compliance, and downstream AI workflows without moving data outside
of Snowflake.

Key benefits include:

- **Lower transcription costs:** Process large volumes of audio and video content more efficiently across
  production AI and analytics workloads.
- **Scalable media analytics:** Transcribe calls, podcasts, interviews, advertisements, meetings, and video
  content directly from Snowflake stages.
- **Multilingual transcription:** Automatically detect languages and transcribe multilingual and mixed-language
  recordings with improved accuracy.
- **Video file support:** Transcribe MP4 and other supported video formats for advertising analytics,
  sponsorship intelligence, and media workflows.
- **Downstream AI analysis:** Combine transcripts with Cortex AI Functions to summarize conversations, extract
  entities, identify brands, analyze sentiment, and power search and reporting experiences.

For more information, see [AI\_TRANSCRIBE](/sql-reference/functions/ai_transcribe) and
[Cortex AI Multimodal](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/ai-multimodal).
