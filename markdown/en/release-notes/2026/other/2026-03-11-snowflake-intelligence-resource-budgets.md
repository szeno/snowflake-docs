# Mar 11, 2026: Resource budgets for Snowflake CoWork

Snowflake now supports resource budgets to control credit spending for Snowflake CoWork. Resource budgets let you define
a monthly spending limit and take automated actions, such as revoking access, when spending exceeds your limits.

For more information, see [Resource budgets for Snowflake CoWork](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/snowflake-cowork/cowork-resource-budgets).
