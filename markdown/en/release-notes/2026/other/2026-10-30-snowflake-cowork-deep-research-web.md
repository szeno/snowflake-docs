# October 30, 2026: Deep Research in Snowflake CoWork can research the web

Deep Research in Snowflake CoWork now researches the web as part of an investigation when the agent has [web search](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-agents-manage#label-cortex-agents-web-search) enabled. Web findings are cited in the report alongside findings from your data. The web research has no access to your data. Without web search, investigations use only your data.

For more information, see [Overview of Snowflake CoWork](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/snowflake-cowork).
