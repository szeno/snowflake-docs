# Jul 21, 2026: CoCo Desktop - *General availability*

CoCo Desktop is now generally available. CoCo Desktop is a native Mac and Windows desktop
application that brings CoCo’s full agentic capabilities into an IDE-like surface, with a file
editor, integrated terminal, agentic AI loop, agentic browser, agentic notebook, and deep
Snowflake awareness.

Because it runs against your Snowflake account and honors your existing roles and access
controls, you can develop, explore data, and administer your account with natural language
without stepping outside your governed environment.
