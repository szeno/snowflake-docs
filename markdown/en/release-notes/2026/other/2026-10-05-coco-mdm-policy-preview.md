# Oct 05, 2026: Enforce CoCo policy with MDM (*Preview*)

Enforcing CoCo policy with Mobile Device Management (MDM) is now available in preview.
Administrators can deliver account restrictions, allowed authentication methods, and minimum
version requirements for CoCo CLI and CoCo Desktop through a macOS configuration profile, Windows
Group Policy or Intune, or `managed-settings.json`. Policy delivered through MDM can’t be changed or
removed by standard users.

For more information, see [Enforce CoCo policy with MDM](/user-guide/cortex-code/mdm-policy).
