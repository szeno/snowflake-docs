# Sep 23, 2026: Account posture reporting in the Trust Center (*Preview*)

With this preview release, the **Overview** tab in the Trust Center includes an **Account posture** section. This
section aggregates Trust Center monitoring activity for your account over a period that you select, showing
scanning status, open findings, and at-risk entities. You can also generate a PDF posture report that covers
scanner enablement, configuration changes, and violation and detection activity for the period.

For more information, see
[Review account posture](/user-guide/trust-center/using-the-trust-center#label-trust-center-account-posture).
