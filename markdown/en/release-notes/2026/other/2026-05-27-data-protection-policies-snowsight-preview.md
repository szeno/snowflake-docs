# May 27, 2026: Data protection policies in Snowsight (*Public Preview*)

With this preview release, you can use the **Governance & security** » **Data protection policies** area in Snowsight to create
data protection policies from the **Dashboard**, list and manage policies on the **Policies** tab, and view protected objects on the
**Objects with policies** tab.

For more information, see [Manage data protection policies in Snowsight](/user-guide/data-protection-policies-snowsight).
