# Oct 16, 2026: Grok 4.7 and GLM 5.3 models (*Preview*)

The `grok-4.7` and `glm-5.3` models are now available in Public Preview in AI Functions, Cortex Inference,
Cortex Agents, Snowflake CoWork, and Snowflake CoCo.

Both models support cross-region inference with `CORTEX_ENABLED_CROSS_REGION` set to `ANY_REGION`, `AWS_GLOBAL`, or
`AWS_US`.

For more information, see [Models and regional availability for Cortex AI Functions](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/aisql-regional-availability),
[Model availability](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-rest-api#label-cortex-complete-llm-model-availability), and
[Cortex Agents](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-agents).
