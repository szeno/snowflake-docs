# Nov 11, 2026: Openflow Snowflake Deployments in AWS government regions (*General availability*)

Openflow Snowflake Deployments are now generally available in the following AWS government regions:

- US East (Commercial Gov - N. Virginia)
- US West (Commercial Gov - Oregon)
- US Gov East 1 (FedRAMP High Plus)
- US Gov West 1 (FedRAMP High Plus)

Openflow isn’t yet FedRAMP-authorized. For account availability and authorization status, contact your
Snowflake account team.

For more information, see [AWS government regions](/user-guide/data-integration/openflow/about-spcs#label-openflow-spcs-aws-government-regions).
