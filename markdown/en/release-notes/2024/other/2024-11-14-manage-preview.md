# November 14, 2024 — Manage account preview features — *General Availability*

Snowflake is pleased to announce the General Availability of account level management of preview features.
With this capability, account administrators can enable, disable and view the status of [preview features](/release-notes/preview-features) within their account.

For additional details, see:

- [SYSTEM$DISABLE\_PREVIEW\_ACCESS](/sql-reference/functions/system_disable_preview_access)
- [SYSTEM$ENABLE\_PREVIEW\_ACCESS](/sql-reference/functions/system_enable_preview_access)
- [SYSTEM$GET\_PREVIEW\_ACCESS\_STATUS](/sql-reference/functions/system_get_preview_access_status)
