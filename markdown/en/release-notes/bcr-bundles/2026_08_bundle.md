# 2026\_08 Bundle (Preview)

## Bundle history

Attention

The current status of this bundle is dependent on the completion of the
10.37 release (October 7-13, 2026, subject to change). Behavior changes described
in this page might not become available in your account(s) until the
completion of the release.

To determine the current version for your account, see
[CURRENT\_VERSION](/sql-reference/functions/current_version).

To determine the current status of bundles in your account,
see [SYSTEM$SHOW\_ACTIVE\_BEHAVIOR\_CHANGE\_BUNDLES](/sql-reference/functions/system_show_active_behavior_change_bundles).

1. Planned to be introduced, **Disabled by Default**, in the 10.37 release
   (October 7-13, 2026, subject to change); once introduced, account admins can enable for testing.
2. Status planned to change in a subsequent release to **Enabled by Default**; however,
   this schedule is subject to change.
3. Status planned to change in a later release to **Generally Enabled**; however,
   this schedule is subject to change.

behavior change beforehand. The change list may not include all changes in a release, for example, last minute or emergency changes.
