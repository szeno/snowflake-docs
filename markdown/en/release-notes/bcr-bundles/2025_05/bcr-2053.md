# Cortex Search Services: Replication of existing services

Attention

This behavior change is in the 2025\_05 bundle.

For the current status of the bundle, refer to [Bundle History](/release-notes/bcr-bundles/2025_05_bundle#label-behavior-change-bundle-2025-05-status).

Before the change:
:   [Cortex Search](/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-search/cortex-search-overview) Services created before July 1, 2025 are not
    [replicated](/user-guide/account-replication-intro) even when they are included in a [replication group](/user-guide/account-replication-intro#label-replication-and-failover-groups).

After the change:
:   When this behavior change bundle is enabled, Cortex Search Services created before July 1, 2025 are
    replicated when they are in a replication group.

This behavior change does not affect Cortex Search Services created on or after July 1, 2025.

Ref: 2053
