# Direct shares: Resharing enabled within the consumer’s organization (Pending)

Attention

This behavior change is in the 2026\_06 bundle.

For the current status of the bundle, refer to [Bundle history](/release-notes/bcr-bundles/2026_06_bundle#label-behavior-change-bundle-2026-06-status).

When this behavior change bundle is enabled, a consumer who has imported a database from a
provider’s direct share can [reshare](/collaboration/reshare-listings) that data by default,
but only with accounts in the consumer’s own organization. Cross-organization resharing of direct
shares isn’t supported.

Before the change:
:   A consumer couldn’t reshare data imported from a provider’s direct share unless they built
    additional pipelines or dynamic tables to copy the provider’s data to reshare to another account.
    Attempts to reshare the data as is failed with the error `PROVIDER_DATA_IS_NOT_ALLOWED_TO_RESHARE`.

After the change:
:   By default, consumers can now reshare incoming data from a provider’s direct share within their own
    organization, without building additional data-copy pipelines. Cross-organization resharing of
    direct shares isn’t supported; to reshare data across organizations, share the data through a
    [listing](/collaboration/reshare-listings) instead.

    There’s no per-share control to disable resharing for an individual direct share. To disable
    resharing, [convert the direct share to a listing](/collaboration/provider-listings-creating-publishing#label-convert-direct-share-listing),
    which lets you control resharing through the [`resharing` field](/progaccess/listing-manifest-reference#label-listing-api-manifest-resharing)
    in the listing manifest, or contact [Snowflake Support](/user-guide/contacting-support) for further
    assistance.

[Resharing](/collaboration/reshare-listings) lets a consumer share data they’ve imported with other
accounts, and has been available for listings, where providers control it through the listing
manifest. This change brings direct shares to parity with listings, so consumers can reshare data
imported from a direct share with other accounts in their organization.
