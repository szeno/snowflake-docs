# Cortex model deprecations for May 2026

The following model deprecations are planned for May 2026. Customers using these models in
AI\_COMPLETE, CORTEX.COMPLETE, Agents API, or Snowflake CoWork are expected to transition
to an alternative model in advance of the deprecation date to avoid disruption.

| Model | Deprecation date | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Opus 4 | May 1, 2026 |  |
| OpenAI OSS 120B | May 1, 2026 |  |
| OpenAI OSS 20B | May 1, 2026 |  |
| Claude Sonnet 4 | May 19, 2026 | For Cortex Agents, Cortex Analyst, Cortex Code, Snowflake CoWork \*only\* |

model will fail after the deprecation date.
