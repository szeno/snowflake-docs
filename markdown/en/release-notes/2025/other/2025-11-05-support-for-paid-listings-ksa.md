# Nov 05, 2025: Support for paid listings in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA) (*General availability*)

Providers can create and offer paid listings in KSA. For a complete list of countries where providers can offer paid listings, see [Who can provide paid listings](/collaboration/provider-becoming#label-monetization-provider-region-support).

Similarly, consumers can access paid listings in KSA. For a complete list of countries where consumers can access paid listings, see [Confirm your billing location is supported](/collaboration/consumer-listings-paying#label-monetization-consumer-region-support).
