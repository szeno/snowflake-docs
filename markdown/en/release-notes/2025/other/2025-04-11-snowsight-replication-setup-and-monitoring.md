# Apr 11, 2025: Snowsight replication configuration and monitoring (*General availability*)

With this release, configuring and monitoring replication with Snowsight is generally available. You can perform
the following actions with Snowsight:

- [Create a replication or failover group](/user-guide/account-replication-config#label-create-replication-failover-group-using-ui).
- [Modify a replication or failover group in a source account](/user-guide/account-replication-config#label-modify-replication-failover-group-using-ui).
- [Pause or resume a replication schedule in a target account](/user-guide/account-replication-config#label-pause-or-resume-replication-failover-group-using-ui).
- [Drop a replication or failover group](/user-guide/account-replication-config#label-drop-replication-failover-group-using-ui).
- [Promote a target account to serve as the source account](/user-guide/account-replication-failover-failback#label-replication-promote-failover-group-using-ui).
- [Monitor replication, including the progress of refresh operations and replication history](/user-guide/account-replication-monitor).
- [Create a primary and secondary connection](/user-guide/client-redirect#label-create-primary-connection-ui).
- [Modify target accounts for a connection](/user-guide/client-redirect#label-modify-target-accounts-for-connection-ui).
- [Monitor Client Redirect connection details](/user-guide/client-redirect#label-monitor-client-redirect-in-ui).
- [Drop a connection](/user-guide/client-redirect#label-drop-connection-using-ui).

For more information about replication, see [Introduction to replication and failover across multiple accounts](/user-guide/account-replication-intro).
